School Projects:

Junior Design Project – Greenhouse Climate Regulation System: A collaborative, short-term engineering design project focused on applying the fundamentals of the engineering design process. The objective was to develop an automated climate control system for small, residential greenhouses to maintain optimal environmental conditions for plant growth.

    Our team designed and constructed a scaled prototype capable of monitoring temperature and humidity under simulated climate conditions. Using sensor-based controls, the system automatically adjusted a greenhouse window and regulated a ventilation fan to maintain the desired internal temperature. This project provided hands-on experience in system design, prototyping, environmental monitoring, and automated control while strengthening our teamwork and problem-solving skills.
    Senior Design Project – Smart Inhalation Spirometer:

    A year-long, multidisciplinary engineering design project completed in collaboration with an external industry sponsor, Lung Technologies. The objective was to develop an enhanced electronic spirometer capable of tracking successful device usage and providing reminders to encourage consistent patient use and support effective respiratory therapy. As one of two mechanical engineers on the team, I was responsible for designing the main spirometer housing using SolidWorks. My focus was creating an ergonomic, user-friendly design that accommodated external electronic components while maintaining proper airflow. Key design challenges included incorporating sponsor-requested modifications to reduce the device's maximum inspired volume and overall size. I utilized SolidWorks for CAD development and SLA 3D printing for prototyping, testing, and design refinement. This project provided hands-on experience in mechanical design, product development, rapid prototyping, and interdisciplinary collaboration.
